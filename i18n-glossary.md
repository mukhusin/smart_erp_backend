# Smart ERP Kiswahili glossary

One word per idea, used on every screen, message, SMS and PDF. Terms without a natural Kiswahili word stay in English
(VAT, PAYE, TIN, VRN, NSSF, NHIF, WCF, SDL, HESLB, LPO, SMS, PDF, CSV, QR, Debit, Credit). Please review and mark changes.
